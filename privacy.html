GINI Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

GINI Studio (the website and the Android/iOS app) lets you pick a garment, colour, print and size, see a mockup, and send your order to GINI on WhatsApp. This page explains what happens to your information.

What we collect

The app does not create accounts, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not send any data to our servers. The name, city and order details you type stay on your phone until you tap Order on WhatsApp.

When you order

Tapping Order on WhatsApp opens WhatsApp with your order message filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. After that, your message, name and phone number are handled by WhatsApp (see the WhatsApp privacy policy) and by GINI only to confirm, make and deliver your order.

Photos and sharing

If you tap Share / save mockup, the app makes an image of your design and opens your phone's share menu. The image goes only where you choose to share or save it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
